Common questions

Is an annual backflow test the same as a repair?

No. An annual test measures whether the assembly still passes. A repair or rebuild is separate work after a fail. Replacement is another job. Ask which of those is quoted.

Who receives the Atlanta test report?

Local water authorities such as those in Fulton, Cobb, DeKalb, and Gwinnett counties require the certified test report. Ask the tester whether they submit the report and whether that filing is included. This site is not a water authority.

Who licenses backflow testers in Georgia?

Georgia requires Backflow Prevention Assembly Tester (BPAT) certification. Ask for the certification number on the report. This site is not the Georgia EPD or a licensing board.

Why is a repair quote higher than the annual test?

A passing annual test is one service. Parts, rebuild kits, and replacement assemblies are different work. Ask for the test result first. This site does not set prices.

Do I have to replace an assembly that failed once?

Ask whether a repair kit can restore the assembly versus replacement, and get the fail reason in writing. Confirm any retest and refile after repair.
